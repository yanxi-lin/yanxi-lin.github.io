Selected work

IEEE ISCAS 2026

DPAM: A Dual-Path Protected Approximate Multiplier for Efficient and Reliable Neural Network Acceleration

Chao Chen, Yanxi Lin, Haoan Yin, Jilong Zhang, Yan Li, and Xiaoyang Zeng

A fault-tolerant approximate multiplier based on logarithmic arithmetic. The design combines approximate triple modular redundancy in the exponent path with a self-detecting truncated mantissa adder to balance hardware efficiency and reliability.
